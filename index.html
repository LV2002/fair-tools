Frequently Asked Questions

For students & parents

1. Is the fair really free?

Yes. Entry is free for students & parents. Just register to get your entry pass.

2. Where is the venue & how do I get there?

The fair takes place at Holiday Inn, NH Bypass, Vennala, Kochi, on the main highway. It is about 45–50 minutes from Cochin International Airport (COK) & 15–20 minutes from Ernakulam South/North railway stations.

3. What are the timings?

The fair runs from 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM on Saturday, 12 December 2026. You can come at any time during the day.

4. What should I bring?
  • Your QR entry pass (a screenshot or the PDF works)
  • Your school or college ID card
  • Your sketchbook or portfolio, if you have one
  • A notebook for your questions
5. Can my parents or friends come with me?

Yes, please bring your parents. When you register, tell us how many people are coming (up to 4) & they can all enter on the same pass.

6. I lost my pass. What do I do?

Register again with the same mobile number or email & your original pass will be shown again. You can also WhatsApp us on +91 98954 84651.

25+ leading design & architecture institutions offering programs in Fashion, Product Design, Communication Design, UI/UX, Animation, Interior Design & Architecture. Register to receive updates as the exhibitor list is finalised.
